Frequently Asked Questions

Working With Us

Who is RapidRise Advisory for?

Large established organisations, including major retailers, logistics operators, REITs and commercial estate owners. We advise on how physical infrastructure, particularly energy generation and storage, can be structured to protect operational performance and create long term commercial value.

I run a large business. How is this relevant to us?

If your organisation operates a substantial physical estate, whether fulfilment centres, distribution hubs, retail stores, car parks, or logistics networks, there is a growing structural question about whether parts of that estate should now be treated as energy infrastructure rather than passive overhead.

We help you answer that question with commercial rigour, screening your sites, identifying where on-site generation and storage carry operational value, and structuring the commercial and planning pathway to act on it.

We already purchase renewable electricity. Why would we need this?

Renewable procurement is a tariff arrangement. The power still arrives through the same local grid connections, subject to the same capacity constraints and peak import costs. As electrification accelerates across your operations, whether through fleet charging, automated processing, or expanded operating hours, the question shifts from energy cost to energy resilience.

We help identify the specific sites where behind-the-meter generation and storage can protect throughput, charger uptime, and service continuity in ways that procurement alone cannot.

Do you work with startups?

Not through RapidRise Advisory. Startups and growing companies in AI, deep technology and defence technology are served by our sister brand, RapidRise.io.

How Engagements Work

What does an engagement look like?

Every engagement begins with a Commercial Architecture Review, a fixed-fee diagnostic that screens your estate, identifies priority sites, and produces a board-ready assessment of where on-site energy infrastructure carries genuine operational and commercial value.

The diagnostic is designed to take two to four weeks. If the findings justify it, we then work with you on planning, commercial structuring, and delivery strategy, drawing on a specialist bench of grid connection, solar and storage design, and energy procurement experts.

What is a Commercial Architecture Review?

It is our entry-point diagnostic. We screen selected assets across your estate to establish the realistic generation, storage, and flexibility potential of each site, the planning route available under current permitted development rights, the revenue and resilience value of acting, and the commercial structure required to proceed.

The deliverable is a prioritised site assessment with recommended next steps, designed to support internal decision-making at board level.

Do you offer ongoing advisory beyond the diagnostic?

Yes. Where the diagnostic reveals material value, we offer ongoing advisory support on planning, commercial structuring and delivery strategy.

We also manage specialist delivery partners for grid connection assessment, system design, and commercial structuring on your behalf.

Sectors and Expertise

Which sectors do you work in?

Major retailers, grocery chains, logistics and fulfilment operators, commercial real estate investment trusts, and any business operating a substantial UK physical estate with exposure to energy cost, grid constraint, or fleet electrification.

Do you work with property companies and REITs?

Yes. Our work is designed for listed and private property owners. We assess whether parts of their estate, particularly retail parks, distribution centres, and assets with large surface car parks, carry strategic infrastructure value that conventional property classification does not capture. The lens is energy generation, battery storage, tenant energy resilience, and the impact on asset valuation and rental income durability.

Do you install solar panels or battery storage?

No. We are a strategic advisory practice, not an installer or EPC contractor.

We provide the commercial architecture, site screening, planning pathway, and commercial structuring that determines whether and where installation makes sense.

Where a project proceeds to deployment, we can introduce you to vetted specialist partners for design, engineering, and installation.

Getting Started

How do we begin?

We start with a short discovery call to understand your situation and whether there is a fit. If there is, the entry point is a Commercial Architecture Review: a fixed-fee diagnostic that screens selected assets and produces a board-ready assessment of where on-site energy infrastructure carries genuine operational and commercial value. It is designed to take two to four weeks. The review stands alone as a useful deliverable regardless of what comes next.

How much does a Commercial Architecture Review cost?

Pricing is scoped to the complexity of the estate and the number of sites under review.

For listed REITs and major retailers, the review is priced as a fixed fee in the range of GBP 8,000 to GBP 15,000.
